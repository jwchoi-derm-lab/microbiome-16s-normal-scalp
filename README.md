---
  
  ## Overview
  
  The scalp harbors a complex microbial ecosystem, and bacterial dysbiosis has been implicated in scalp dermatitis and hair disorders. However, a unified reference for the normal scalp microbiome remains lacking due to small, heterogeneous studies.  

**Purpose:**  
  This study aims to comprehensively characterize the normal scalp microbiome across different sampling modalities and demographic groups, providing a robust reference for future research and clinical applications.

---
  
  ## Features
  
  - Integration of multiple public 16S rRNA datasets for meta-analysis
- Comparative analysis across hair, swab, and tissue samples
- Visualization of taxonomic diversity and composition
- Generation of summary tables for demographic and microbial profiles
- Reproducible analysis pipeline in R
